# Spec — B2B only

**Date:** 2026-08-15
**Status:** approved by owner, implemented same day
**Closes:** [go-live-checklist.md](../go-live-checklist.md) §3

## Goal

Answer the customer-model question that has blocked the legal pages since
2026-07-26: **the shop sells to businesses, research institutions, laboratories
and institutional buyers only. No contracts are concluded with consumers
(§ 13 BGB).**

The model is the sister project `peptidebestellung.de`, which is live with this
position. It was read as prior art on 2026-08-15 and its structure is followed
deliberately, not copied verbatim — its texts assume a German seller, and this
company is a Virginia LLC.

## Why this is a set, not a wording change

A B2B position is only defensible if the shop actually restricts to businesses.
Publishing "kein Verbraucherwiderrufsrecht" on a checkout that still accepts
consumers is **worse than the consumer version**: a consumer who buys without a
valid Widerrufsbelehrung gets roughly twelve months to withdraw instead of
fourteen days (§ 356 Abs. 3 BGB). The gate and the text ship together or not at
all.

The sister site enforces it in three places, and all three are reproduced here:

1. a required confirmation at checkout that the buyer is ordering commercially;
2. an AGB clause under which the customer confirms Unternehmer status
   (§ 14 BGB) on placing the order;
3. legal pages that state the restriction rather than implying it.

## Scope

| File | Change |
|---|---|
| `pages/widerruf.astro` | Consumer Widerrufsbelehrung and Muster-Widerrufsformular replaced by the B2B notice: no consumer shop, no consumer withdrawal right, no discretionary return, returns only after written approval |
| `pages/agb.astro` | § 2 gains the § 13 BGB exclusion and the § 14 BGB confirmation; § 7 becomes a B2B notice; § 12 gains the Gerichtsstand clause, permissible only towards Kaufleute |
| `pages/kasse.astro` | New **required** business-and-research confirmation; the existing legal checkbox drops Widerruf |
| `pages/retouren-reklamation.astro` | The withdrawal section stops deferring to an open customer model |
| `pages/about.astro`, `pages/contact.astro` | The `/widerruf/` link stops describing a consumer withdrawal right |

## Non-goals

Stated explicitly so scope does not drift:

- **VAT is not settled by this.** B2B removes the *gross-price display*
  obligation (PAngV protects consumers), but says nothing about what tax a US
  seller owes on goods shipped to German business customers. § 4 keeps a
  placeholder, reworded away from "Kleinunternehmerregelung", which is a
  German-seller concept that never applied here.
- **No legal review.** The pages keep `draft` and their banner. The sister site
  being live is evidence the structure works, not that this text has been read
  by a lawyer (§4).
- **No pricing change.** Prices stay as displayed; only what may be *said* about
  tax changes.
- **No backend enforcement.** The confirmation is a checkout-form requirement,
  the same mechanism as the existing legal checkbox. The store API does not
  verify customer type, and this spec does not add that.
- **Catalog data (§5) and the confirmation email (§6) are untouched.**
